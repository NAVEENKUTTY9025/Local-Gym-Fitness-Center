Floor Standards

THE ATHLETE CODE OF CONDUCT

To protect focus and training safety, every member agrees to uphold these floor standards.

Rule 01 // Re-Rack Every Plate

Return Equipment Immediately

Every calibrated plate, dumbbell, cable attachment, and clip must be placed back on its designated tree or rack after completing your final set.

Rule 02 // Respect The Zone

No Tripods Or Prolonged Calls

Phone calls must be held in the reception lounge. Extended tripod filming that intrudes on fellow athletes' privacy is strictly prohibited.

Rule 03 // Activewear Attire

Closed-Toe Athletic Shoes Only

Clean training footwear and active shirts are mandatory throughout the facility. Barefoot lifting is only allowed during calibrated deadlift setups on platforms.

Rule 04 // Work-In Culture

Allow Peers To Share Machines

During peak floor hours, allowing fellow members to work-in between your rest intervals is required to ensure optimal floor efficiency.
