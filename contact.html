FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before visiting our training grounds.

Do I need an appointment for a facility tour? ▼

Walk-in tours are always welcome during staffed hours (06:00 AM - 09:30 PM). If you'd like a guided biometric walkthrough with a CSCS coach, please book 2 hours in advance using the inquiry form above.

How do guest day passes work? ▼

First-time local visitors receive a complimentary 1-Day All-Access Pass upon presenting local photo ID. Passes grant full access to barbell decks, classes, and recovery thermal plunge suites.

Is parking validated for members? ▼

Yes, all members and visiting trial guests receive 2.5 hours of complimentary covered parking in our private athletic structure with direct elevator access.

Can I train at any campus with one membership? ▼

Yes. All Pulse Fit memberships include full reciprocal access to the Downtown Flagship, Westside Athletic Campus, and Marina Bay Performance Center.
