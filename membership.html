Membership FAQs

Can I switch between monthly and annual plans at any time? ▼

Yes! You can upgrade your billing cadence directly from your member dashboard. If you upgrade from monthly to annual, you immediately unlock the 20% savings and receive a complimentary InBody 770 scan.

How does the 14-day money-back guarantee work? ▼

If within 14 days of sign-up you decide Pulse Fit is not the right fit for your training, simply tap "Request Cancellation" in your member dashboard or email support. You will receive an immediate full refund with no questions asked.

Can I bring workout partners with guest passes? ▼

Standard and Premium members receive monthly guest passes in their Pulse Fit app. You can generate an instant QR pass for a guest directly on their phone for full floor and sauna access.
